From discovery and research to full-scale production, scaling a laboratory requires efficient data management to ensure compliance, reproducibility, and operational continuity. Small-medium business (SMB) research labs transition significantly as they grow, and without the right systems in place, these changes can lead to inefficiencies and data silos. To remain efficient and productive, lab systems and structures must adapt to their evolving needs.
